Are “sure odds” real? What 2 odds tips can and can’t do

If you’ve searched for “sure 2 odds today”, you’ve probably seen a lot of promises. Here’s the version with the maths in it.

The blunt answer

No. There’s no such thing as a sure football tip. Odds of 2.00 mean that the market thinks the outcome happens about half the time. A pick that wins half the time is a coin flip with a bookmaker’s margin attached, and no amount of confident wording changes that.

What the numbers say about streaks

If a site claims it landed ten 2.00 picks in a row, remember how rare that would be. Ten wins in a row at 50% each is 1 in 1,024, about 0.1%. When someone posts a perfect month, either they have been very lucky, or they’ve posted the wins and quietly deleted the losses. The second is far more common.

Fixed matches and “VIP” offers

Some sellers claim to have inside information on fixed games, usually for a fee. This is a well-known scam. If they really had it, they’d bet it themselves rather than sell it for a subscription. Never pay for a “sure” tip, and never send money to someone who promises that you can’t lose. We do not sell tips, and we do not offer fixed matches.

What an honest tipster looks like

  • Shows losses next to wins, for every pick.
  • Gives the odds and the chance behind each pick, so you can judge it.
  • Publishes a record over a long enough period to mean something, not a screenshot from a good Saturday.
  • Talks about risk and stake size, not just returns.

That’s the standard we try to hold ourselves to. Every pick on the site gets a ✓ or ✗ after the final whistle, and the track record shows the 30-day figures. Our single shot page shows the replay of earlier days, including the days it lost.

So what are 2 odds tips good for?

They’re a convenient shortcut. A 2 odds slip is built from short-priced games with the best combined chance we can find, and it will still lose roughly one time in two. Used with a small stake, that’s entertainment with a clear price. Used as an income plan, it’s a fast way to lose money.

Frequently asked questions

Are sure odds real?

No. No football tip is guaranteed. Odds of 2.00 imply about a 50% chance, so a 2 odds pick loses about as often as it wins.

Are fixed matches real?

Sellers who claim to have fixed matches are almost always scammers. Never pay for a tip that promises a certain win.

How can I judge a tipster?

Look for published losses as well as wins, the odds and chance behind each pick, and a record over many weeks rather than a few good days.
