What is a half-time/full-time bet? All nine outcomes explained

Two predictions in one bet: who leads at the break, and who wins at the end. Both have to be right.

How it works

You pick a result for half time and a result for full time, so the options are written like Home/Home, Draw/Away or Away/Draw. There are nine combinations: three half-time results times three full-time results. The bet wins only if both halves of your pick are correct.

How likely is each outcome?

Here is a worked example for a typical home favourite, expected to score about 1.5 goals against 1.1, with the simple assumption that 45% of the goals arrive before half time. It is a model, not a record of real games.

HT/FTChanceFair odds
Home/Home28.0%3.6
Draw/Home16.0%6.2
Draw/Draw15.8%6.3
Away/Away15.7%6.4
Draw/Away10.5%9.6
Home/Draw5.0%20.2
Away/Draw5.0%20.2
Away/Home2.4%41.0
Home/Away1.7%60.5

The three ways for the home side to win add up to about 46%, which matches a normal home-win price near 2.15. Splitting that win into three half-time stories is what makes each single outcome so much less likely. Even the favourite’s Home/Home pick lands only about 28% of the time.

Why Draw/Home and Draw/Away are popular

Many matches are level at half time, and a stronger team often breaks the deadlock later. That is why Draw/Home is the second most likely outcome above, at 16%. It is also why those prices look attractive, and why the bookmaker prices them carefully.

The catch

As with correct score, the margin on this nine-way market is usually bigger than on a normal 1X2 bet, so the price you are offered is shorter than the fair odds above. Checking how odds convert to chances shows how much you need to win to break even. We do not publish half-time/full-time picks, because our data has no half-time prices or half-time scores to check them against.

If you want to back a team but fear a draw, draw no bet and double chance are simpler markets.

Frequently asked questions

What does HT/FT mean in betting?

HT/FT stands for half time/full time. You predict the result at the break and the result at the end of the game, and both must be correct.

How many outcomes are there in a half-time/full-time bet?

Nine: each of the three half-time results (home, draw, away) combined with each of the three full-time results.

What is the most likely HT/FT outcome?

For a home favourite it is usually Home/Home, at roughly 28% in a simple model. For an evenly matched game, Draw/Draw is often near the top.

Is HT/FT easier than a normal match result bet?

No. You need two things to be right, so each outcome is less likely than the plain 1X2 result it contains.
