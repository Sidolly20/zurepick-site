# ZurePicks 2.0: what changed

## Design (zp.css was rewritten from scratch)
- Bright, calm, spacious: warm white background, white cards, ink text, one orange accent. Green appears only on picks and wins.
- One font family (Figtree) for a faster load.
- The home page now opens with the games: a short title, one line of stats, a "games worth a look" list, then the day tabs and the match board. The 2/3/5 odds card, single-shot card, glowing pick-of-the-day card and hero illustration are gone from the home and day pages.
- Bet types live in one tidy "Bet types" dropdown in the header (every market, 2/3/5 odds, single shot and the tools). They are also still in the footer and the Slips tab.
- Match cards, match pages, league pages, slips pages, live, tools, notebook, about and guides all use the same new look.

## Nothing removed from the site
- All pages, links, sitemap entries, the tab bar, the header and the footer are unchanged in purpose. The slips pages (2, 3, 5 odds, single shot) still exist.
- The SEO work from the previous round (titles, descriptions, structured data, visible FAQs, author byline, honest sitemap dates) is untouched.

## Files changed in this round
zp.css, build-pages.mjs, manifest.webmanifest
