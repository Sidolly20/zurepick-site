When under 2.5 goals is worth a look

Under 2.5 is for cagey football: two cautious sides, a decider where nobody wants to lose first, or a mismatch where the favourite takes the lead and then manages the game. It is also the natural counter to a goals-heavy day. When everybody is chasing overs, the under is sometimes the better-priced side.

Where it goes wrong

The big mistake is treating a 0-0 as the target. Under 2.5 loses to a single 2-1, a penalty and an own goal, and a late goal can flip a match you were winning for eighty minutes. Late kick-offs in warm weather and games with nothing at stake are worth a second look too, because open games are common in both.

A worked example

If a game has Over 2.5 at 2.30 and Under 2.5 at 1.62, the raw chances are 43.5% and 61.7%, which add to 105%. Remove the 5% margin and the under is about 59%, just short of our bar. At 2.45 and 1.55 the under rises above 60% and the game would be listed.
