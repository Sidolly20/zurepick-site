About ZurePicks

A free football predictions site built around one idea: show the numbers, then show whether they were right.

What we publish

Every day ZurePicks lists the day’s football fixtures with bookmaker odds, the market’s implied chance for each result and a pick in the main markets: 1X2, double chance, over/under 1.5 and 2.5 goals and both teams to score. Scores update as games are played, and every pick turns green or red at full time.

Why you can trust the numbers

Every finished pick is stored and counted on the track record page, wins and losses alike. We explain exactly how picks are made on How it works, and we never promise sure wins, because nobody can.

Quality over quantity

We don’t predict every game. We publish a pick only when it clears our quality bar, and games without a strong enough pick are left off the board.

Fixtures without odds

For games with no bookmaker prices, ZurePicks uses a team-record model built from tracked results. It only publishes when it has enough games for both teams and the pick clears the same bar, and it is labelled so you can tell it apart from odds-based picks.

Who runs ZurePicks

A. S. JayeFounder and editor
In football predictions since 2019Every result posted

I have worked in football predictions since 2019, back when there were no APIs and no automation. Fixtures, odds and results were gathered and checked by hand, one game at a time. ZurePicks carries what those years taught me: nothing is published that I could not trace back to a bookmaker’s price.

After years of watching tips sites hide their losses and shout about “sure” wins, I started ZurePicks to do the opposite. Every result is posted on the track record, win or lose, so you can judge for yourself. Every pick is tied to real odds, every result goes on the track record, win or lose, and a game with no strong pick is left empty instead of padded.

I don’t sell fixed matches, VIP groups or guaranteed wins, because after this long in the game I know none of them are real. What you get here is honest numbers, explained plainly, so you can make your own call.

— A. S. Jaye
