Betting bankroll and staking: the boring guide that saves money

Nobody posts about this part, but it’s the part that decides whether betting stays fun.

Set a budget first

Decide an amount you could lose without it touching rent, food or bills, before you place a single bet. Treat it as the price of entertainment, like a cinema ticket.

Keep stakes small

Many careful bettors keep each stake to around 1–2% of their betting budget. That way a cold streak stings but doesn’t end your month.

Never chase

After a loss, the urge to win it back with a bigger bet is the single most expensive habit in betting. If you feel it, close the app.

Know when to stop

If gambling stops being fun, hides from friends or family, or eats money you need, step away and speak to a support service in your country. See our responsible gambling page.

No pick, ours or anyone else’s, is a sure thing.

Frequently asked questions

What is a bankroll?

It’s the amount of money you’ve set aside for betting, separate from money you need for everyday life.

How much should I stake per bet?

There’s no perfect number, but many people keep each stake to a small slice of their budget, around 1–2%, and use less on long-shot slips.
