J1 League predictions today: the early-morning football in Nigeria

Japan is eight hours ahead of Nigeria. A 7pm kick-off in Japan is 11am in Nigeria, so J1 League games are morning or midday football on a Nigerian clock. That suits people who like to check results before lunch.

The league is known for organised teams, high fitness and a lot of travel. Because it gets less coverage than Europe’s big leagues, odds can move less, and team news is harder to find. Stick to the chance shown on each card and use a smaller stake than you would for the Premier League. See kick-off times in Nigerian time.
