Over/Under 3.5 goals explained: how often do games reach four?

Over 3.5 is the goals line for games that go wild. Here is how often they actually do.

What the line means

Over 3.5 wins when the match has four goals or more in total. Under 3.5 wins with three or fewer. The half goal means a refund is impossible, as with over and under 2.5. For the basics of goal lines, see over/under goals explained.

How often does it happen?

In the 9,393 finished games we have tracked, 34% had four goals or more, and the average game had 3.0 goals. How often it happens depends heavily on the game. For a game where about 2.6 goals are expected, a simple model gives Over 3.5 a 26.4% chance (fair odds 3.79) and Under 3.5 a 73.6% chance (fair odds 1.36). Where more goals are expected, as in many of the games we track, Over 3.5 gets closer to a one-in-three chance.

What that means for the price

Over 3.5 usually pays between 2.50 and 4.50 depending on the teams, because it loses more often than it wins. Under 3.5 pays short odds, often near 1.30, and at that price you need to win about 77% of the time to break even. Short prices feel safe, but a bet that loses one time in four or five needs a very careful price. The maths is in value betting explained.

When it is worth a look

Over 3.5 only makes sense in games where both teams attack and defend badly, and where the bookmaker’s Over 2.5 price is already short. Under 3.5 looks better as a cautious alternative to Under 2.5, but it pays so little that a single surprise result wipes out many wins.

Why you will not find Over 3.5 picks here

The site collects odds for the 1.5 and 2.5 lines, plus both teams to score, so there are no 3.5 picks yet. We would rather say that than invent a price. The Over 2.5 and Over 1.5 pages cover the lines we do track.

Frequently asked questions

What does Over 3.5 goals mean?

The bet wins if the match finishes with four or more goals in total, counting both teams.

How often do games have more than 3.5 goals?

In the games we track, 34% finished with four or more goals, so Over 3.5 loses more often than it wins.

What odds should Over 3.5 goals have?

If the real chance is about 26%, fair odds are near 3.8. Bookmakers usually offer a little less than that.

Is Under 3.5 safer than Under 2.5?

It wins more often, but it pays much less. A single game with four goals ends the bet, so the short price needs a high win rate to be worth it.
