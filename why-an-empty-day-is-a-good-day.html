Why an empty day is a good day

Why ZurePicks leaves games without a tip, what the quality bar is, and why a short list beats a padded one.

By A. S. Jaye · 12 Nov 2026

Some days the board is short. Every so often it has almost nothing. People write to ask whether something is broken. It is not. That is the quality bar doing its job.

What the bar is

For each game we take the bookmaker’s odds, remove the margin, and see how likely each outcome is. A pick is published only when that chance clears a line: 60% for a straight result, 75% for double chance, and so on. Green dots above the line are published. The grey ones are games we looked at and left alone.

Why not fill the gaps

Because a tip nobody believes in is worse than no tip. If we lowered the line to fill every day, the average pick would be weaker and the record would suffer, and you would have no way to tell the strong picks from the filler. A short list with a clear reason for every line is more useful than a long list of guesses.

What to do on a quiet day

Use the “show all games” button on the today page to see every game we hold odds for, check live scores, read the guides, or simply skip the day. Skipping is a perfectly good bet.
