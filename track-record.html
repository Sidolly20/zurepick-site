How we track every pick

Each finished match with pre-match odds is stored with the pick we would have made and the final score. Nothing is removed afterwards, wins or losses. The hit rate is simply the share of tracked picks that won, shown by market and by implied chance, so you can see whether higher-chance picks really win more often.

How to read these numbers

A 70% hit rate does not mean every pick wins seven times in ten in any short run. Small samples swing widely, and a run of losses is normal. Use the tables to judge a market over weeks, not a single day. For the reasoning behind this, read why a 70 percent pick still loses and how accurate football predictions are.

A football tipster track record that shows the losses

Search for a transparent football tipster track record and you will notice how rare the real thing is. Most “winning” pages show only the wins. Ours is built the other way round. Every pick published before kick-off is graded after the final whistle, and the table includes the failures. You can read how the grades are made on how it works.

Read the table like this: look at the chance band, then at the strike rate. If 70–79% picks win somewhere around 70–79% of the time, the numbers are doing their job. If the strike rate is far below the band for a long stretch, the method needs fixing, and we would rather you spot that than hide it.

What a good track record looks like, and what it does not

A short run proves very little. Ten wins in a row is luck, and ten losses in a row is also luck. Judge a prediction site by volume, by consistency between promised chance and actual result, and by whether it still shows the bad weeks. Be suspicious of anything that claims 90% or more over a short window.

Frequently asked questions

Does ZurePick hide losing picks?

No. Every finished match with pre-match odds is stored with its pick and result, and the tables count wins and losses alike.

What hit rate counts as good?

It depends on the odds. A 75% hit rate on short-priced picks can still lose money, so look at hit rate together with the odds and the number of picks.

How often is the track record updated?

It updates as matches finish and their results are recorded.

Does ZurePick show its losing predictions?

Yes. Every published pick is graded and stays in the record, whatever the result.

How do I know the track record is not edited?

Picks are published before kick-off and graded from the final score. The results come from the same data feed that drives the live page, and you can check any match yourself.
