What is draw no bet (DNB)?

Fancy a team to win but fear the draw? This market takes the draw out of the picture.

How draw no bet works

You pick one team. If that team wins, you’re paid at the odds. If the game ends in a draw, your stake is returned. If your team loses, you lose the stake. That’s all of it: two possible outcomes with a refund as the safety net.

Where the price comes from

The easiest way to see it is to build the price from ordinary 1X2 odds. Say the home win is 2.10, the draw 3.40 and the away win 3.50. Their raw chances are 48%, 29% and 29%. Remove the draw and keep the other two: home is 48 out of 77, about 62.5%, so the draw no bet price for the home side comes out near 1.60.

That’s the swap you’re making. A 2.10 shot that can be lost on a draw becomes a 1.60 shot that can’t. You’ve paid about 0.50 in odds for the refund.

When it’s a good choice

  • An away team that should be OK but tends to draw a lot.
  • A strong side in a tight game where a single goal could decide it.
  • A team you back to win, but you’re not sure about the opponent’s home record.

When it’s not

If the team you like is a heavy favourite at 1.30, the draw no bet price is tiny and barely worth it. And if the real danger isn’t a draw but a loss, DNB won’t help you at all.

DNB, double chance and 1X2

It’s easy to mix these up. Double chance covers two of three results (home or draw, for example) but gives no refund; you win if either happens. Draw no bet refunds a draw but only wins on one team. The 1X2 market is the plain version: home, draw or away, no safety net. Our double chance guide explains the other half of the comparison.

A note on refunds and accumulators

In an accumulator, a refunded DNB leg is usually treated as odds of 1.00, so the slip carries on with one fewer leg. Check your bookmaker’s rules, because some apps handle it differently. See also the glossary.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to draw no bet if the game is a draw?

Your stake is refunded. You do not win or lose anything on that bet.

Is draw no bet the same as double chance?

No. Double chance wins if either of two results happens and pays no refund. Draw no bet only wins on one team but refunds a draw.

Are draw no bet odds lower than 1X2 odds?

Yes. Because the draw is refunded, the price is shorter than the same team’s 1X2 win price.
