How to read this page

The page is split by what you probably want to know at that moment. Live now lists games in play with the current score and minute. Kicking off in the next 4 hours shows what is about to start and the picks we published for each game, with the percentage the bookmakers’ prices imply. Finished today is the part we care about most: the final score, then one small badge per pick.

A green tick means the pick won, a red cross means it lost. A game can carry up to five picks because we grade every market separately: 1X2, double chance, over/under 2.5, over/under 1.5 and both teams to score. A game with only one badge simply had one pick that cleared our quality line; the others were too close to call and we kept quiet.

Why the scores can lag a little

We would rather tell you than pretend. The site is rebuilt from the data feed roughly every ten minutes, and your browser then reads the newest snapshot every minute. A goal can therefore show up a few minutes late. A game that has passed its kick-off time without a score yet appears as in play with no score, not as a guess.

Where the finished results go

Every graded pick on this page also feeds the track record, which keeps the long-run numbers by market. If you want to see how a particular type of pick has done over a month, start there, then come back here for today. For tomorrow’s games, head to tomorrow’s predictions; for ready-made accumulators see the 2 odds and 3 odds slips.

Frequently asked questions

How often does the live page update?

The site is rebuilt about every ten minutes from our data feed, and your open page checks for the latest snapshot every minute. Scores can be a few minutes behind a live-score app.

What does a green tick or red cross mean?

Each badge is one published pick on that game. Green means it won, red means it lost. We grade every market on its own, so one game can show several badges.

Why does a finished game show no picks?

Because nothing on that game reached our quality line before kick-off. We only publish picks the odds rate at 60% or more (75% for double chance and over 1.5), so some games are left without a tip.

Does ZurePicks show every game in the world?

No. We show the fixtures our data provider supplies with odds. On quiet days that can be a short list, and we would rather show fewer games than invent a pick.
