Why the combined chance matters

The odds multiply, and so do the chances. Three games at 70% each land together only about 34% of the time. If the total odds imply a higher chance than you really have, the slip isn’t worth it. Read how accumulators work, or grab a ready-made 2 odds, 3 odds or 5 odds slip.

Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate accumulator odds?

Multiply the odds of every game. Three games at 1.45 give 1.45 × 1.45 × 1.45, about 3.05.

What is the combined chance of an accumulator?

Multiply each game’s chance. Three 70% picks land together about 34% of the time.
