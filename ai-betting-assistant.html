How the AI football picker works

The assistant reads live bookmaker odds for every game that has not kicked off, strips out the bookmaker’s margin and keeps only outcomes the market rates as strong: 60% or more for match result, over 2.5 and both teams to score, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. It then tests thousands of combinations and returns the one that lands closest to 2 or 3 total odds with the best combined chance.

AI betting tips today and tomorrow

Today’s slips use only today’s games and tomorrow’s slips use only tomorrow’s. Nothing from another day is mixed in. Prices for games further ahead appear as bookmakers publish them, so tomorrow’s slips get stronger through the day. AI betting assistant FAQ

How does the AI betting assistant pick games?

It turns live odds into implied chances, keeps only strong picks, then searches combinations for about 2 or 3 total odds with the highest combined chance.

Is the AI football picker accurate?

No tipster can promise wins. A 70% pick still loses about three times in ten. Every result is published on the track record page so you can judge for yourself.

Will today’s slip include tomorrow’s games?

No. Choose Today or Tomorrow first and the assistant only uses games that kick off that day and have not started.
