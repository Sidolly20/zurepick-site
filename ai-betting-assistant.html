Three ways to play, one rule

  1. 2 oddsThe cautious slip. Few games, each one the market rates highly, adding up to about 2.00.
  2. 3 oddsSame method with more room. It uses up to six games to reach about 3.00 without leaning on one risky leg.
  3. Single shotNo slip at all. The AI searches every market for the single outcome closest to a coin flip that the bookmakers still favour, and gives you a backup from another game. Two games at most.

How the AI football picker works

The assistant reads live bookmaker odds for every game that has not kicked off, strips out the bookmaker’s margin and keeps only outcomes the market rates as strong: 60% or more for match result, over 2.5 and both teams to score, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. It then tests thousands of combinations and returns the one that lands closest to 2 or 3 total odds with the best combined chance. Legs from the same league are slightly marked down, because games in one competition tend to move together.

What the single shot does differently

A slip needs every leg to land. A single shot needs one thing to go right. For the single shot the AI skips the slip search and reads every market it has for each game: match result, double chance, goals lines, both teams to score, plus extras like team goals and first-half goals. It keeps outcomes priced between 1.90 and 2.20 (widening to 1.85–2.30 if the day is thin), then ranks them by chance and takes the best one per game. You get a shot and a backup. We stop at two because a longer list is a menu, not a pick.

Be realistic about it. At odds of 2.00 the market itself says about 50%, so a single shot loses nearly as often as it wins. The value is in picking the best-priced coin flip rather than in any promise. Read the single shot page for the replay of earlier days, wins and losses together.

AI betting assistant FAQ

How does the AI betting assistant pick games?

It turns live odds into implied chances, keeps only strong picks, then searches combinations for about 2 or 3 total odds with the highest combined chance.

Is the AI football picker accurate?

No tipster can promise wins. A 70% pick still loses about three times in ten. Every result is published on the track record page so you can judge for yourself.

Will today’s slip include tomorrow’s games?

No. Choose Today or Tomorrow first and the assistant only uses games that kick off that day and have not started.

What is a single shot?

One game and one outcome priced near 2.00, picked from every market the assistant has, with a backup from a different game. It is never more than two games, and you bet them separately.

Can the single shot be at 3 odds?

We keep it near 2.00 on purpose. A single at 3.00 has about a one in three chance, so it loses twice as often as it wins. For 3 odds, use the slip, which spreads the risk across several shorter-priced games.

Is it free?

Yes, free with no sign-up. Shuffle as often as you like for a different slip, or the next-best single shot.
