Football tips for SportyBet, Bet9ja, MSport and 1xBet: how to use them

Whichever app you use, the method is the same: choose the games first, then rebuild the slip and check the prices before you confirm.

Written by A. S. Jaye, founder and editor · Reviewed 20 November 2026

The one rule that works on every bookmaker

ZurePicks is an independent tips site. It does not run a bookmaker and does not take bets. Our picks come from published odds and a calculated chance, and you place the bet yourself in the app you trust. The steps are the same everywhere: add the games to the slip on our page, copy it, then find each game in your app and compare the price.

Why the odds are not identical everywhere

Every bookmaker sets its own prices and its own margin. A game that is 1.85 on one app can be 1.78 on another. A small difference matters when you stack legs: on a three-leg slip, a 0.05 drop on each leg cuts the total by roughly 8% to 10%. Always trust the price on your own screen, not the number in a screenshot.

SportyBet

Search for the match by team name, open the market that matches our pick (1X2, double chance, over/under or GG/NG) and add it to the slip. If the line is not listed, skip that leg instead of replacing it with a different market. Free betting tips for SportyBet are only useful if the legs on your slip match the legs we showed.

Bet9ja

Bet9ja lists GG and NG in the goals section and double chance under its own heading. If you are looking for “bet9ja sure predictions today”, remember that no list is sure: pick two or three legs with the highest chance and keep the stake small.

MSport

MSport has strong coverage of African leagues and plenty of football markets. Use the search for a team or league and match the market name carefully, because wording can differ slightly between apps.

1xBet

1xBet usually has the widest list of leagues and the widest range of markets. That helps with smaller competitions, but it also makes it easy to add legs you did not plan. Keep to the picks on your list. On “1xbet sure odds today” searches, the same warning applies: nothing is sure, and the combined chance on the 2 odds slip tells the real story.

What about free booking codes?

A booking code is a saved slip that works on one bookmaker only and stops working once the first game starts. Codes shared by strangers can be old, can have different odds from the original or can include legs you would not choose. If you use one, open it, read every leg and drop what you do not agree with. More in how to add our slip to your betting app.

A short safety list

  • Never pay anyone for a “fixed match”. See fixed matches and sure odds scams.
  • Set your stake before you look at the potential win.
  • Use only licensed bookmakers in your country and only if you are 18 or older.
  • If betting stops being fun, read responsible gambling.

Before you deposit anywhere

Check that the bookmaker is licensed where you live, that it has clear rules on withdrawals and that it offers limits and self-exclusion. Our guide on how to check whether a betting site is safe walks through it. Also read the rules on void legs and early cash out, because they change how an accumulator is settled. See cash out explained.

Frequently asked questions

Does ZurePicks work with SportyBet, Bet9ja, MSport and 1xBet?

You can copy our slip and rebuild it in any of them. We do not run or own a bookmaker.

Why is the same game priced differently on each app?

Each bookmaker sets its own odds and margin, so prices differ slightly.

Do you publish booking codes?

We show slips you can copy. A booking code only works on one bookmaker and expires at kick-off, so rebuilding the slip yourself is safer.
