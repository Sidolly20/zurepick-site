How it works

Where the percentages come from

For each market we take the bookmaker’s decimal odds and convert them into implied probabilities (one divided by the odds). Those add up to more than 100% because the bookmaker builds in a margin, so we scale them back to 100%. The result is the chance you see. There is no secret model and no human tipster: the pick is simply the outcome the market rates as most likely.

Markets we cover

Match result, double chance, over/under 2.5 goals, over/under 1.5 goals and both teams to score. A market only appears when a bookmaker has priced it.

How picks are settled

Odds are collected before kick-off and stored. When the match finishes, the final score decides whether each pick won or lost, and the result goes into the track record. Cup games that go to extra time are settled on the score the data provider reports, which can differ from some bookmakers’ rules.

Limits

Odds-based chances are estimates. A 70% pick still loses about three times in ten, and a short price can win often and still lose money over time, which is why the track record shows profit in units as well as win rate. Coverage depends on the free data we receive, so some leagues have no picks. Nothing here is financial advice or a guarantee.
