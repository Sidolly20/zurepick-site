How ZurePicks works

No hidden tricks. Here is exactly where the numbers come from and what they can and cannot tell you.

1. Fixtures and odds

Fixtures, scores and pre-match odds come from football data providers and are refreshed several times a day by an automatic job.

2. Implied probability

Decimal odds convert to a probability by dividing 1 by the odds. Bookmaker prices add up to more than 100% because of their margin, so ZurePicks rescales each market back to 100%. That rescaled figure is the percentage you see.

3. The pick

The pick is the outcome with the highest rescaled probability in that market. For double chance, it is the pair of results with the highest combined probability.

4. Results

When a match finishes, the pick is marked won or lost and stored permanently for the track record.

5. The quality bar

Not every game gets a pick. A pick is published only when its implied chance is 60% or higher (75% or higher for double chance and over 1.5). In our own tracked results, 1X2 picks under 60% won roughly 45–50% of the time, while picks at 60% or higher won far more often. This is a small sample and past results do not guarantee future ones, so the bar will be reviewed as the record grows.

6. Games without odds

For games with no bookmaker prices we use a team-record model: a Poisson model built from each team’s tracked goals scored and conceded, using only results played before the match. It is held to the same quality bar and labelled “team-record model”.

Limits

The picks are market-based estimates. They do not use private information, and no site can promise winners. Football is unpredictable, so stake responsibly.
