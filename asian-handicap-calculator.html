Asian handicap calculator

Pick the handicap line your team was given, enter your stake, the odds and the final score, and we’ll show the exact result, including half wins and half losses.

How an Asian handicap works

The handicap is added to your team’s goals before the result is judged. With a -0.5 line your team must win outright. With +0.5 it wins the bet if it wins or draws. A whole-number line such as -1 can end in a push, where the stake is simply returned.

Quarter lines: half the stake on each side

Lines ending in .25 or .75, such as -0.25 or +0.75, split your stake across the two nearest half-lines. A bet on -0.25 puts half the stake on 0 and half on -0.5, so a draw returns half your stake (a half loss) and a one-goal win pays in full. Because of this, you can see a half win or a half loss, which a normal win-or-lose bet never gives you. 18+ only, play responsibly.

Frequently asked questions

What does -0.25 mean in an Asian handicap?

Half your stake is on 0 (draw no bet) and half on -0.5. A win pays in full, a draw loses half your stake, and a defeat loses it all.

What does +0.75 mean?

Half your stake is on +0.5 and half on +1. A win or draw pays in full, a one-goal defeat wins half and returns half, and a heavier defeat loses.

What is a push?

A push happens on a whole-number line when the adjusted score is level. The stake is returned and there is no profit or loss.

Is an Asian handicap safer than 1X2?

It removes the draw as a separate outcome, but it is not safer by default. The odds change to match the line, so risk and reward move together.
