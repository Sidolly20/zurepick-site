How to read football form (and why last 5 games can mislead)

Form strips like WWDLW are everywhere in betting previews. They are useful, but only if you know what they leave out.

Written by A. S. Jaye, founder and editor · Reviewed 13 November 2026

What the letters mean

Form is a team’s recent results, usually the last five games, written from the oldest to the newest or the other way round, depending on the site. W is a win, D a draw and L a loss. WWWDW looks excellent, and LLLDL looks poor. That is the easy part.

Why five games is a small sample

Five results can be shaped by a single lucky goal or a red card. A team that went WWWWW may have scored late in three of them. Treat form as a hint, and not as a fact. Longer runs, such as the last ten games, are steadier, and so is points per game across a full season.

Four things the strip hides

Opposition. Five wins against the bottom of the table is not the same as five wins against the top. Check who they played.

Home and away. Many teams look very different on the road. A side with great home form and weak away form needs to be judged for the venue of the next game.

Scorelines. Winning 1-0 five times says something different from winning 4-0 five times. Goals scored and conceded show how convincing the form was.

Team news. Injuries, suspensions and fatigue after midweek games can change a team before the strip catches up.

Using form the sensible way

Combine form with odds. The bookmaker’s price already reflects form, injuries and much else, so form on its own rarely finds a bargain. It is better at explaining why a game is priced the way it is. Our odds guide shows how to turn a price into a percentage, and each match page on ZurePicks shows form alongside the odds when we have the data.

Frequently asked questions

How many games count as form?

Most sites use the last five. Ten games give a steadier picture, and a full season’s points per game is steadier still.

Is good form a reason to bet on a team?

Not by itself. The odds already include form, so a team in good form is often priced as a favourite. You need the price to be fair as well.

Does home or away form matter more?

For the next game, use the form from the same venue as well as the overall run.

What does WWDLW mean?

Win, win, draw, loss, win, across the last five games.
