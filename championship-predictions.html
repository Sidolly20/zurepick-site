Championship predictions today: accumulators and BTTS

The English Championship has 24 clubs and 46 games each, which means a lot of fixtures and a lot of variety. Gaps between teams are smaller than at the top level, so results are less predictable and draws are common. That is why the league is popular for accumulators and BTTS.

With so many games, form changes quickly. A manager change can shift a team’s outlook in a week. Look at the chance behind each price, keep your legs few and read how accumulator bets work before you stack the Championship’s tricky results.
