What is a double chance bet?

Double chance is football betting with a safety rail. A safer rail, not a guaranteed one.

The three options

1X means the home team wins or draws. X2 means the away team wins or draws. 12 means either team wins, so the bet loses only on a draw. Cover two outcomes and you win more often.

The catch

The extra cover is priced in. Double chance odds are usually much shorter, often between 1.10 and 1.40, so one bad result can wipe out the profit from several good ones.

Where it fits

It’s handy as a leg in a slip when a favourite looks strong but you worry about a draw. We only publish double chance picks at 75% or more, because shorter odds need a higher strike rate to be worth it. See today’s double chance tips.

Frequently asked questions

Is double chance safer than 1X2?

It wins more often because it covers two results, but it pays less. Over time, shorter odds need a high strike rate to stay profitable.

What does 12 mean in a double chance bet?

It means either team wins. You lose only if the match ends in a draw.
