Champions League predictions tonight: midweek nights explained

Champions League games are usually played on Tuesday and Wednesday evenings. In the European winter the common kick-offs of 6:45pm and 9:00pm CET are the same clock time in Nigeria, because CET and WAT are both UTC+1. In summer, European clocks move forward and the games are an hour earlier on a Nigerian clock.

Cup nights behave differently from league weekends. Teams play for aggregate results, rotation is common, and a defensive draw can suit both sides. Use the odds and the chance on each card to see where the market is confident, and read kick-off times in Nigerian time so you never miss a start.
