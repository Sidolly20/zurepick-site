How we choose the safest football tips today

We take every game with published bookmaker odds, remove the bookmaker’s margin, and keep the outcome with the highest chance. A game makes this page only if that chance is 75% or more and the price is at least 1.15, across Home, Draw and Away, Over 1.5, 2.5 and 3.5 goals, and both teams to score. Double chance is left out on purpose, because its prices are usually too small to be worth a bet.

Banker tips and safe bets: what they really mean

People search for “banker” or “sure” tips, but nothing in football is certain. A banker is simply the pick we rate strongest. Our banker of the day is the single best of these, and every result, win or lose, stays on the track record.

Sure football predictions today: the honest version

People type “sure football predictions today” because they want certainty. Football does not offer it. What it does offer is a range: some outcomes are far more likely than others, and a careful filter can find them. Our safest-tips page applies a simple rule. A game is listed only when the bookmakers’ own prices, with the margin removed, imply a chance of 75% or better.

That is a strong pick, not a sure one. A 75% outcome fails one time in four. If you stack four of them in a slip, the whole slip lands only about 32% of the time. If a site tells you a pick is 100% sure, read are sure odds real? before you send them any money.

How to use safe tips sensibly

  • Use one or two as legs, not ten.
  • Short prices pay little: at odds of 1.30 you must be right about 77% of the time just to break even.
  • Check the date and the kick-off time. A tip for a game that already started is no use.
  • Never chase a loss with a bigger stake. See bankroll and staking.

Frequently asked questions

What are the safest football tips today?

They are the games on this page: each has a chance of 75% or more and odds of at least 1.15, taken from the bookmakers’ prices with their margin removed.

Are safe tips guaranteed to win?

No. A 75% chance still loses about one game in four. We post every result so you can judge for yourself.

What is a banker tip?

A banker is the pick we rate as strongest. Our banker of the day is chosen every morning and its result is kept on the pick of the day page.

Are safe tips guaranteed?

No. Even a 75% pick loses about one game in four. Nothing in football betting is guaranteed.

Why do the safest tips have such short odds?

Because the market already knows they are likely. A higher chance means a shorter price, which is the trade-off behind every bet.
