Privacy policy

Short and plain: what this site stores, what other services see and what we do not do.

The short version

ZurePicks has no accounts, no sign-up and no payments. We do not ask for your name and we do not sell personal information, because we do not collect it ourselves.

What stays on your device

The slip builder remembers the games you add to your slip, and a small setting remembers which data source the site should use. Both are saved in your browser’s local storage on your own device. We do not set cookies for advertising or tracking.

What other services see when you visit

Loading a page involves a few outside services. Like any web server, each of them can see your IP address and basic browser details:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep ordinary server logs.
  • Render hosts the live data service that updates scores and odds on the page.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces.
  • football-data.org supplies team crest images.

Their own privacy policies explain what they keep.

Visit counting

We do not currently run visitor analytics. If we add a privacy-friendly visit counter, we will say so here first.

Links and sharing

The Telegram and WhatsApp buttons open those apps with a prepared message. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it, and those services have their own policies.

If you contact us

If you write to us by email or Telegram, we keep your message only as long as we need it to reply or to fix a mistake you reported.

Age

This site is for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we add a service that handles visitor data, we will update this page first. Last updated: November 2026. Questions? Use the contact page.
