What to message us about

Wrong score or result? Tell us the match and date and we’ll check it. Ideas for new leagues, features or guides? Send them, because many of the pages on this site started as a message. Want to partner with us or advertise? Keep it clear and legal and we’ll talk. Rights holders and clubs can ask for a badge or logo to be removed and we’ll act quickly.

How fast we reply

This is a small, independent site, so replies usually take a day or two, sometimes longer on big match weekends.

Staying safe

ZurePicks never takes bets, deposits or payments, and never sells “fixed matches”. Anyone asking you for money in our name is not us. Please don’t send passwords or card details to anyone, including us. See our responsible gambling page, and remember: 18+ only.
