Jackpot predictions: why 13-game pools almost never pay

A jackpot pool asks you to get a whole list of games right. The prize is big because the chance is tiny. Here are the numbers.

Written by A. S. Jaye, founder and editor · Reviewed 20 November 2026

How a jackpot pool works

You predict the result of a fixed list of matches, often 13 or 17, usually a home win, draw or away win for each. If you get them all right you win the top prize. Many pools also pay smaller prizes for getting most of them right, but the rules, prices and prizes differ from one operator to another, so read yours.

The odds of guessing

Each game has three outcomes. With 13 games, pure guessing gives 3 × 3 × … 13 times, which is 3 to the power of 13 = 1,594,323. So you have about a 1 in 1.6 million chance. With 17 games it is 129,140,163, about 1 in 129 million.

What if you are good at predicting?

Say you pick the right result 60% of the time, which is already very good for 1X2. Getting all 13 right is 0.60 to the power of 13, about 0.13%, or roughly 1 in 766. At 70% it is about 1%, or 1 in 103. At 70% over 17 games it falls to about 0.23%, 1 in 430. Almost nobody keeps 70% accuracy across a mixed list of games, because pool lists often include evenly matched and unpredictable fixtures.

Why jackpot tips for sale are a trap

  • If someone could reliably hit a 13-game jackpot, they would play it themselves, not sell tips.
  • “Sure jackpot” offers, screenshots of past wins and WhatsApp groups that ask for payment are classic scams. See fixed matches and sure odds scams.
  • A “prediction” for every game is still only a guess for each one. See are sure odds real.

Looking for this week’s jackpot games?

The list of games in a jackpot pool is set by the operator and changes every round, so we cannot publish it. Check it on the operator’s own site or app. For the matches themselves, our weekend predictions and safest tips today show the odds-based chance for each game we cover, which is a better guide than a jackpot “tip”.

If you still want to play

Treat it like a lottery ticket. Spend only a small amount you would not miss, do not chase losses, and do not borrow to play. The pool is about the thrill of a long shot, not an income. You can compare short odds on single matches with our accumulator calculator, which shows how quickly chances shrink as you add games.

Frequently asked questions

What are the odds of winning a 13-game jackpot?

If you guess every result, 1 in 1,594,323. Even at 70% accuracy per game, the chance of getting all 13 is about 1 in 103.

Can anyone predict a jackpot reliably?

No. Even very good accuracy on single games shrinks quickly when you must get every game right.

Are paid jackpot tips real?

Almost always no. Selling tips for a jackpot you could win yourself is a warning sign of a scam.

Do jackpot pools pay for fewer correct results?

Many do, but the rules and prizes differ by operator, so check the terms before you play.
