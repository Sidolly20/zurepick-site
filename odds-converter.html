How odds convert

Decimal odds of 2.50 equal 3/2 in fractional and +150 in American. The implied chance is 100 ÷ the decimal odds, so 2.50 means 40%. That figure includes the bookmaker’s margin, so the true chance is a little lower. Learn more in how to read football odds.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert fractional odds to decimal?

Divide the top number by the bottom one and add 1. So 3/2 becomes 1.5 + 1 = 2.50.

What chance do decimal odds of 2.00 imply?

100 ÷ 2.00 = 50%, before the bookmaker’s margin is removed.
