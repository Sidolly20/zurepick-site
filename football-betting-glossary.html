Football betting glossary

Betting talk can sound like another language. Here are the terms you’ll meet most, explained without the jargon.

Accumulator (acca, parlay)
One bet that combines several games. Every leg must win, but the odds multiply together.
Bankroll
The money you’ve set aside for betting, separate from money you need for everyday life.
Banker
A pick someone considers very strong. No pick is certain, so treat the word as a label, not a promise.
BTTS (GG)
Both teams to score. It wins if each side scores at least once, whatever the final score.
Bet slip
The list of selections you’re about to bet on, with the total odds and return.
Booking code
A short code some betting apps create so a slip can be shared and rebuilt in the same app.
Bookmaker margin (overround)
The built-in fee in the odds. It’s why the chances for all outcomes add up to a little over 100%.
Cash out
Settling a bet before it finishes, for a price the bookmaker offers.
Correct score
A bet on the exact final score, such as 2–1. Hard to land, so the odds are long.
Decimal odds
The total return per 1 staked, stake included. Odds of 2.00 return 2.
Double chance
Covers two of the three results (1X, X2 or 12) in one bet. Safer, so the odds are shorter.
Draw no bet
Back a team to win; if the game is drawn, you get your stake back.
Fractional odds
Odds shown as profit over stake, such as 3/2. It equals 2.50 in decimal.
Handicap
A head start given to one team to even out the game, such as Team A −1 or Team B +1.
Implied probability
The chance the odds suggest. Divide 1 by the decimal odds: 2.00 means 50%.
In-play (live) betting
Betting on a game while it’s being played, with odds that change as the game moves.
Odds-on
Odds shorter than evens (below 2.00 in decimal), meaning the result is rated more likely than not.
Over/Under
A bet on whether total goals finish above or below a line, such as 2.5.
Stake
The amount you bet.
Value bet
A bet where the odds are bigger than the real chance deserves. It’s hard to spot and never a guarantee.
Void bet
A bet cancelled and refunded, for example if a game is called off.
1X2
The match-result market: 1 is a home win, X is a draw, 2 is an away win.
