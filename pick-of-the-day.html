How the pick of the day is chosen

Each morning we look at the day’s games that haven’t started and pick the 1X2 result the market rates highest, as long as the price is worth taking (odds of at least 1.30, and a chance of 65% or more). The pick is saved once and never changes, so the record can’t be edited after the fact. Read more in how it works, or see the weekly review.
