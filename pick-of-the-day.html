How the pick of the day is chosen

Each morning we look at the day’s games that haven’t started and pick the 1X2 result the market rates highest, as long as the price is worth taking (odds of at least 1.30, and a chance of 65% or more). The pick is saved once and never changes, so the record can’t be edited after the fact. Read more in how it works, or see the weekly review.

Banker of the day and bet of the day: what it means here

A banker is the pick you trust most on the day. People search for “banker of the day football” and “bet of the day football” to get one clear selection without scrolling through a hundred games. We choose ours from the strongest published price on the board, and the result is posted whatever happens.

We use the word banker carefully. In betting slang it can sound like a guarantee. It is not. It is simply the best-rated option on a given day, and even the best-rated option loses sometimes. Read what a banker bet really is for the full picture, and keep the stake as small as you would for any other bet.
