What is expected goals (xG), and does it help with betting?

xG appears in every match report now. Here is what the number means and what it cannot do.

The idea in one minute

Expected goals gives every shot a value between 0 and 1, which is the chance that a shot like it ends in a goal. A tap-in from six yards might be worth 0.7. A long shot from outside the box might be worth 0.03. Add up the values for all shots in a match and you get that team’s xG.

Why people care

Scorelines are noisy. A team can lose 1-0 after creating several clear chances and conceding one fluke. xG tries to show who played better regardless of finishing luck. If a team keeps winning with low xG, some analysts expect results to cool off. If a team keeps losing with high xG, they may expect improvement.

What goes into the number

Providers build xG models from historical shots. Common inputs are the distance and angle of the shot, whether it was a header or a foot, and the kind of pass that created it. Different providers use different models, so the same match can have slightly different xG figures depending on the source.

Limits you should know

xG does not know about goalkeepers having a great or terrible day. It does not capture every detail of a chance, such as how many defenders were in the way, unless the model includes tracking data. It also needs a decent number of games before it says anything reliable, as a single match can mislead.

xG and betting

xG can help you question a team’s recent results, but the bookmaker’s odds already include this kind of analysis, so it is not a shortcut to profit. ZurePicks does not use xG. Our picks come from bookmaker odds and a results-based team model, as explained on how it works. Treat any stat as one input and not as a promise, and read how accurate are football predictions? for the bigger picture.

Frequently asked questions

What does xG mean in football?

xG stands for expected goals. It estimates how likely each shot is to become a goal, based on factors such as distance and angle.

Is a high xG a good sign?

It suggests a team created good chances. Over several games it is a better guide to performance than the scoreline alone.

Does xG predict match results?

It describes past performance and can hint at future form, but it cannot predict a single game.

Does ZurePicks use xG?

No. Our picks are built from bookmaker odds and a team-record model built from tracked results.
