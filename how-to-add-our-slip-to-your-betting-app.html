How to copy our slips into your betting app

Found a slip you like? Here’s how to rebuild it in your own betting app without mistakes.

Step by step

1. Open the slip and note each game, the market and the odds.

2. In your betting app, search for the first game by team name. Check the date and the league, because teams sometimes meet in more than one competition.

3. Find the matching market. Names vary between apps: our “Over 2.5” may be “Total goals over 2.5”, and “Both teams to score” may appear as “GG/NG”. Our glossary translates the common ones.

4. Tap the odds to add it to your bet slip, then repeat for each game.

5. Before you stake, check the total odds and the potential return.

Why your odds may differ

The odds on our pages come from one bookmaker’s feed at one moment. Your bookmaker may price the same game differently, and prices move before kick-off. If your odds are clearly lower than ours, the pick may no longer be worth backing, so don’t force it.

Booking codes

Some apps turn a slip into a short code you can share. We don’t publish bookmaker codes, because they only work on one app and expire quickly. You can build your own slip from ours and share your code with friends.

Keep stakes small, and read about staking first. 18+ only: play responsibly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I paste a ZurePicks slip into my betting app?

Not directly. Each bookmaker uses its own system, so you rebuild the slip by tapping the same games and markets in your app.

Why is my bookmaker’s total different from the slip total?

Because bookmakers price games differently and prices change. Always trust the total shown in your own app before you stake.
