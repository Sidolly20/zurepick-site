The sweet spot

A 3 odds slip is where most people feel at home: enough of a return to be worth the walk, not so many legs that one stray corner ruins the evening. Think three games around 1.45, or two short prices and one bolder one.

How we build the 3 odds slip

We look at every game with a published price that day, keep the picks the market rates at 60% or more (75% for double chance and over 1.5), then test the combinations and keep the one that lands closest to 3.00 with the best combined chance. Fewer legs usually means a better chance, so we start small and add games only when we have to.

Using it sensibly

Stake an amount you could shrug off. Remember, three 70% picks together land about 34% of the time, which is why every slip shows its combined chance. Want a different mix? 3 odds daily predictions and the “3 odds banker” idea

The phrase “sure 3 odds banker today” mixes two things that do not belong together. A banker is a trusted single pick. A 3 odds slip is a combination of several. What we can do is build the 3 odds slip from the picks with the best chance and show you the combined number honestly. Three 70% picks land together about 34% of the time, so a loss is more likely than a win, and the price pays for that.

A 3 odds slip is where many people settle because it feels balanced: a decent return without a monster list of legs. We keep the number of games as low as we can, because a three-leg slip with higher-chance picks beats a five-leg slip made of weaker ones.

Daily 3 odds rollover: a calmer way to do it

Rolling a 3 odds slip day after day multiplies the risk. At roughly a one-in-three chance per day, a three-day winning streak happens about 4% of the time. If you want a routine, use a fixed daily stake from a bankroll you set in advance, and let the wins and losses even out. The stake size calculator helps you pick a sensible amount.

Frequently asked questions

How many games are in a 3 odds slip?

Usually three or four. We start with the fewest games that can reach 3.00 because fewer legs means a better combined chance.

What are the chances of a 3 odds slip winning?

Three 70% picks land together about 34% of the time. That’s why each slip shows its combined chance next to the odds.

Does the 3 odds slip use tomorrow’s games today?

No. Today’s slip uses only today’s games and tomorrow’s slip uses only tomorrow’s, so nothing from another day is mixed in.

Where do the picks come from?

From the bookmakers’ own prices. We strip out their margin and back the outcome they rate most likely. The percentage is that market-implied chance. It isn’t a secret formula and it isn’t a guarantee.

Why do some games have no pick?

Because a coin flip isn’t a tip. We only publish picks at 60% or more for 1X2, over/under 2.5 and BTTS, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. In our tracked results, picks below that lost far more often, so we leave them out.

What are the odds of a 3 odds slip winning?

It depends on the legs. Three 70% picks win together about 34% of the time. The slip page shows the combined chance for the current build.

Is there a sure 3 odds banker today?

No pick is sure. The slip uses the strongest available picks, but any one leg can lose and that loses the slip.
