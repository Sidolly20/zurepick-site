The sweet spot

A 3 odds slip is where most people feel at home: enough of a return to be worth the walk, not so many legs that one stray corner ruins the evening. Think three games around 1.45, or two short prices and one bolder one.

How we build the 3 odds slip

We look at every game with a published price that day, keep the picks the market rates at 60% or more (75% for double chance and over 1.5), then test the combinations and keep the one that lands closest to 3.00 with the best combined chance. Fewer legs usually means a better chance, so we start small and add games only when we have to.

Using it sensibly

Stake an amount you could shrug off. Remember, three 70% picks together land about 34% of the time, which is why every slip shows its combined chance. Want a different mix? Frequently asked questions

How many games are in a 3 odds slip?

Usually three or four. We start with the fewest games that can reach 3.00 because fewer legs means a better combined chance.

What are the chances of a 3 odds slip winning?

Three 70% picks land together about 34% of the time. That’s why each slip shows its combined chance next to the odds.

Does the 3 odds slip use tomorrow’s games today?

No. Today’s slip uses only today’s games and tomorrow’s slip uses only tomorrow’s, so nothing from another day is mixed in.

Where do the picks come from?

From the bookmakers’ own prices. We strip out their margin and back the outcome they rate most likely. The percentage is that market-implied chance. It isn’t a secret formula and it isn’t a guarantee.

Why do some games have no pick?

Because a coin flip isn’t a tip. We only publish picks at 60% or more for 1X2, over/under 2.5 and BTTS, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. In our tracked results, picks below that lost far more often, so we leave them out.
