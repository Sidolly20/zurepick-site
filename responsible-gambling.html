Responsible gambling

Predictions are information, not advice. Betting is for adults aged 18+.

  • Only stake money you can afford to lose and set a budget first.
  • Never chase losses. A bad run does not mean a win is due.
  • Take breaks, and stop if betting stops being enjoyable.
  • Check that betting is legal where you live.

Free support: BeGambleAware.org · GamblingTherapy.org
