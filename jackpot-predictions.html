Jackpot predictions: how to fill a jackpot slip, with a free calculator

Nobody can promise a jackpot win. What you can do is understand the odds, plan your lines and stop paying for “sure jackpot” tips.

Written by A. S. Jaye, founder and editor · Reviewed 20 November 2026

Free jackpot planner

Enter how many games the jackpot has, how many you will cover with two results (a double) or three (a triple), and what one line costs. The planner shows how many lines you are buying, the total cost and the honest chance of getting every game right.

With the default numbers (15 games, 6 doubles, 9 singles at 55%) you buy 64 lines and the chance of a perfect slip is about 1 in 960. Cover more games and the cost doubles with every double, so the chance rises slowly while the bill rises fast. That is the real trade-off in every jackpot.

How jackpots work

A football jackpot asks you to predict the result (home win, draw or away win) of a fixed list of matches, usually 13 to 17, chosen by the operator. Prizes depend on how many you get right, and the biggest prize needs all of them. Formats, prices and prize rules differ by operator and change over time, so always read the rules on the operator’s own page before you play.

A sensible way to fill a jackpot slip

  1. Rate every game first. Use the win chances on the 1X2 page and the weekend page. Games with a favourite above about 65% are your natural singles.
  2. Expect draws. Roughly a quarter of matches end level, so in 15 games three or four draws is normal. A slip with no draws is unusually optimistic. See draw predictions.
  3. Double the close games. Where no result is clearly ahead, cover two outcomes. The double chance chances show you how likely each cover is.
  4. Triples are expensive. A triple multiplies your cost by three. Use one only on a game that makes no sense at all, such as a derby with both teams in chaos.
  5. Set the budget first. Decide what you can lose, then see how many doubles it buys on the planner above.

What we do not do

ZurePick does not know the operator’s jackpot lists in advance, does not sell “sure jackpot” tips and does not take bets. If someone offers a fixed or guaranteed jackpot for a fee, read fixed matches and sure odds scams and are sure odds real? before you pay anything. For the basics, see jackpot predictions explained.

Play small, play for fun

A jackpot is a lottery with better information. Treat the cost of your lines like the price of a ticket, not an investment, and read responsible gambling if it starts to feel like more than that.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best jackpot predictions?

No one can promise them. The best approach is to rate every game with win chances, play the strongest favourites as singles, double the close games and expect about a quarter of the results to be draws.

How many draws are in a jackpot?

About one match in four ends level, so a 15-game jackpot usually has three or four draws.

How many lines do I buy if I double some games?

Two to the power of the number of doubles, times three to the power of the number of triples. Six doubles is 64 lines.

Does ZurePick give jackpot fixtures or sure jackpot tips?

No. We do not publish operators’ jackpot lists in advance and we do not sell sure tips. We show win chances for football matches that you can use to plan your own slip.
