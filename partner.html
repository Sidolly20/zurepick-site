Advertise & partner with ZurePick

A clean, fast football site built around free predictions, odds tools and honest results. If you run a licensed, responsible brand, here is how we can work together.

Who reads ZurePick

People who follow football and use odds to decide what to watch and what to bet on, mostly on their phones, mostly in West Africa Time. They arrive for today’s games, a 2 odds or 3 odds slip, a single shot, or a plain-English guide, and many come back daily to check results. We publish real traffic numbers to sponsors on request from our own analytics, and we will not quote figures we cannot show.

Ways to work with us

  • Home and league page placement. One clearly labelled sponsor card above the match list, kept visually separate from our tips.
  • Sponsored guide or tool. A clearly marked guide or calculator page on a topic your brand knows, written to our editorial standard.
  • Telegram channel mention. A labelled mention in our daily slips post.
  • Fixed-term sponsorship of a market page (for example 1X2, BTTS or a single league), with the sponsor named in the page header.

Our standards

Adverts are always labelled and never mixed into our tips. Our picks are never changed for a sponsor. We only work with licensed operators and responsible brands, 18+ only, in places where their product is legal. We do not take “fixed match” or guaranteed-win offers, and we will remove any placement that misleads readers.

Frequently asked questions

Do you sell tips or guaranteed wins?

No. ZurePick is free, shows every result win or lose, and never sells fixed matches or sure wins.

Can a sponsor change the picks?

Never. Sponsor placements are separate and labelled, and our picks come only from the data and our published method.

Which brands do you work with?

Licensed, responsible brands only, for adults aged 18 and over, in markets where their product is legal.
