Frequently asked questions

What is over/under 2.5 goals?

Over 2.5 goals wins when the match has three or more goals; Under 2.5 wins with two or fewer. Picks lean on the bookmaker’s goal-line prices and show the implied chance for each side.

How are the picks made?

Each pick is the outcome bookmakers price as most likely, with their margin removed. The percentage is that market-implied chance. It is not a private model and it is not a guarantee.

Why don’t all games have a pick?

We only publish picks where the chance is strong enough: 60% or more for 1X2, over/under 2.5 and both teams to score, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. In our tracked results, lower-chance picks lost far more often, so we leave them out.

What does the percentage mean?

A 70% pick is one the market gives roughly a 70% chance, so it still loses around three times in ten. Use it as a guide to how one-sided a match looks, not as a promise.

How often does the over/under 2.5 goals favourite win?

Over the last 30 days the favourite won 166 of 270 tracked matches (61%). That is a hit rate of the market favourite, not a promise for future games.
