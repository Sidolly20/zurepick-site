Stake size calculator

Enter your betting budget and the percentage you’d like to stake. We’ll show the stake and what a losing run would do to your budget, because that’s the number most people never check.

A sensible starting point

Many careful bettors keep each stake to around 1 to 2% of their betting budget, and less on long-shot slips. Use only money you could lose without it touching rent, food or bills. Read the full staking guide, and remember: 18+ only, play responsibly.

Betting stake calculator and unit size: keep one number small

A unit is a fixed slice of your bankroll, usually 1% to 2%. If your bankroll is 50,000, one unit at 1% is 500. Betting in units means a bad week cannot wipe you out, and a good week does not tempt you into reckless stakes.

To use the calculator, enter your bankroll and the percentage you are comfortable with. The result is the stake for one bet. Keep it the same until your bankroll has changed in a meaningful way. If you want the maths behind bigger or smaller stakes, see the Kelly calculator and bankroll and staking.

A worked example with a losing streak

Say your bankroll is 50,000 and your unit is 1%, which is 500. A run of ten losses in a row, which is rare but possible, costs about 4,800 if you keep the stake at 1% of the shrinking bankroll, leaving about 45,200. The same run at 10% stakes would leave about 17,400, a loss of more than two-thirds. That difference is the whole argument for small stakes.

The calculator shows this for your own numbers. Change the percentage and watch how the damage from a bad run changes. Most people are surprised how little 1% feels like on the day and how much it protects them over a month.

Frequently asked questions

How much of my budget should I stake per bet?

There is no perfect number, but many people keep each stake to around 1 to 2% and use less on long-shot slips.

Why look at losing streaks?

Because every bettor hits them. Knowing what 10 straight losses would do to your budget helps you pick a stake you can live with.

What is a unit size in sports betting?

A fixed percentage of your bankroll, commonly 1% to 2%, used as your standard stake.
