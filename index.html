How to read a ZurePicks match card

Each card is one game. The kick-off time sits on the left in West Africa Time, the two teams are in the middle, and the box on the right shows the score or the match status (not started, live, full time). Under the teams you get the bookmaker’s decimal price and, next to it, the chance that price really implies once the bookmaker’s built-in margin is removed. The highlighted box is our tip, and a card turns green or red after full time so every result stays visible, wins and losses alike. Tap any price to add it to your bet slip.

Which market should you look at?

  • 1X2 (match result) is the classic home, draw or away bet. We only publish it when one side is clearly rated as the favourite. Our published picks in this market won 78% of 354 over the last 30 days.
  • Double chance covers two of the three results (home or draw, away or draw, home or away). Prices are shorter, so we ask for a higher chance before publishing. Our published picks in this market won 84% of 940 over the last 30 days.
  • Over/Under 2.5 goals is about how many goals are scored, not who wins. Good for games where both attacks are better than the defences. Our published picks in this market won 65% of 525 over the last 30 days.
  • Over 1.5 goals needs just two goals in the match and is the safest goals line, at the cost of a smaller price. Our published picks in this market won 83% of 852 over the last 30 days.
  • Both teams to score (BTTS/GG) wins when each side scores at least once, whatever the final result. Our published picks in this market won 61% of 434 over the last 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

What are 2 odds, 3 odds and 5 odds tips?

They’re ready-made accumulator slips. We pick the strongest games of the day and combine them so the total odds land near 2, 3 or 5. Each slip shows its total odds and combined chance, and one tap adds the lot to your bet slip.

Are these football predictions really free?

Yes. Every pick, odds table and result is free, and you never need to sign up.

Where do the picks come from?

From the bookmakers’ own prices. We strip out their margin and back the outcome they rate most likely. The percentage is that market-implied chance. It isn’t a secret formula and it isn’t a guarantee.

Why do some games have no pick?

Because a coin flip isn’t a tip. We only publish picks at 60% or more for 1X2, over/under 2.5 and BTTS, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. In our tracked results, picks below that lost far more often, so we leave them out.

What does the percentage mean?

A 70% pick is one the market rates at roughly 70%, so it still loses about three times in ten. Treat it as a gauge of how one-sided a match looks, not a promise.

Are there sure odds or banker tips?

There are strong picks, but no sure ones. Football is built to surprise people. The strongest picks here are the ones the market rates highest, and every result is on the track record page for you to judge.

What does BTTS or GG mean?

BTTS (also called GG) means both teams to score. It wins when each side finds the net at least once, whatever the final result. A 1-1 draw and a 4-3 win both count.
