How to read a ZurePicks match card

Each card is one game. The kick-off time sits on the left in West Africa Time, the two teams are in the middle, and the box on the right shows the score or the match status (not started, live, full time). Under the teams you get the bookmaker’s decimal price and, next to it, the chance that price really implies once the bookmaker’s built-in margin is removed. The highlighted box is our tip, and a card turns green or red after full time so every result stays visible, wins and losses alike. Tap any price to add it to your bet slip.

Which market should you look at?

  • 1X2 (match result) is the classic home, draw or away bet. We only publish it when one side is clearly rated as the favourite. Our published picks in this market won 75% of 453 over the last 30 days.
  • Double chance covers two of the three results (home or draw, away or draw, home or away). Prices are shorter, so we ask for a higher chance before publishing. Our published picks in this market won 81% of 1068 over the last 30 days.
  • Over/Under 2.5 goals is about how many goals are scored, not who wins. Good for games where both attacks are better than the defences. Our published picks in this market won 64% of 721 over the last 30 days.
  • Over 1.5 goals needs just two goals in the match and is the safest goals line, at the cost of a smaller price. Our published picks in this market won 78% of 747 over the last 30 days.
  • Both teams to score (BTTS/GG) wins when each side scores at least once, whatever the final result. Our published picks in this market won 61% of 617 over the last 30 days.

Accurate football predictions free: what “accurate” can honestly mean

Anyone promising 100% accuracy is selling something. A football pick is a probability, not a prophecy. A 70% pick should lose about three times in ten, and a site that never shows a loss is hiding them. What we can promise is accuracy of a different kind: the percentages are calculated the same way every day, and every result is posted afterwards, wins and losses together, on the track record page.

That is also how you can judge us. Check whether the picks we label 70% really win about 70% of the time over a few weeks. If they do not, you will see it in our own numbers before you see it anywhere else.

Frequently asked questions

What are 2 odds, 3 odds and 5 odds tips?

They’re ready-made accumulator slips. We pick the strongest games of the day and combine them so the total odds land near 2, 3 or 5. Each slip shows its total odds and combined chance, and one tap adds the lot to your bet slip.

Are these football predictions really free?

Yes. Every pick, odds table and result is free, and you never need to sign up.

Where do the picks come from?

From the bookmakers’ own prices. We strip out their margin and back the outcome they rate most likely. The percentage is that market-implied chance. It isn’t a secret formula and it isn’t a guarantee.

Why do some games have no pick?

Because a coin flip isn’t a tip. We only publish picks at 60% or more for 1X2, over/under 2.5 and BTTS, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. In our tracked results, picks below that lost far more often, so we leave them out.

What does the percentage mean?

A 70% pick is one the market rates at roughly 70%, so it still loses about three times in ten. Treat it as a gauge of how one-sided a match looks, not a promise.

Are there sure odds or banker tips?

There are strong picks, but no sure ones. Football is built to surprise people. The strongest picks here are the ones the market rates highest, and every result is on the track record page for you to judge.

What does BTTS or GG mean?

BTTS (also called GG) means both teams to score. It wins when each side finds the net at least once, whatever the final result. A 1-1 draw and a 4-3 win both count.

Are the football predictions on ZurePicks really free?

Yes. Today’s predictions, the 2, 3 and 5 odds slips, the tools and the guides are all free, and you do not need an account.

What does the percentage next to a tip mean?

It is the chance the bookmakers’ odds imply once their margin is removed. A 70% tip means the market rates that outcome as roughly a 7 in 10 chance. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Can I use ZurePicks without signing up?

Yes. There is no registration. Open the page, read the picks and copy the slip if you want it.

Where can I see how your tips have actually performed?

On the track record page, which lists results by market and by chance band, including the losses.
