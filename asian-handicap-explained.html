Asian handicap explained: lines, half wins and pushes

An Asian handicap gives one team a head start so the match is closer to an even contest. Here is how every kind of line settles.

Written by A. S. Jaye, founder and editor · Reviewed 20 November 2026

The idea in one minute

The bookmaker adds or subtracts goals from a team before the result is judged. A team given -1 starts the game one goal down for betting purposes, and a team given +1 starts one goal up. You then bet on which side beats the handicap. The goals are only added in the bet, not in the real match score.

Whole, half and quarter lines

Half lines (-0.5, +1.5) can never push. -0.5 means your team must win. +0.5 means your team wins the bet if it wins or draws. +1.5 means it wins the bet unless it loses by two or more.

Whole lines (-1, +1, 0) can end in a push. If you back a team at -1 and it wins by exactly one goal, the adjusted score is level, so your stake is returned. A line of 0 is the same as draw no bet.

Quarter lines (-0.25, -0.75, +0.25, +0.75) split your stake between the two nearest lines. A bet on -0.25 puts half the stake on 0 and half on -0.5. If the team wins, both halves win. If it draws, the 0 half is returned and the -0.5 half loses, so you lose half your stake. If it loses, you lose everything.

A worked example

You stake 100 at odds of 1.90 on a team at -0.75. It wins 1-0. Half the stake sits on -0.5 and wins: 50 × 1.90 = 95. The other half sits on -1 and pushes, so 50 comes back. You receive 145 for a 45 profit. That is a half win. If it had won by two goals, both halves would win and you would collect 190.

How it differs from 1X2

A normal 1X2 bet has three outcomes: home, draw, away. An Asian handicap removes the draw as a separate result by shifting the line, so it often has only two outcomes, and the odds on each side sit closer to even money. That is why the prices look like 1.90 and 1.95 instead of 1.50 and 6.00. It does not make the bet safer, because the odds change to match the line.

Common mistakes

  • Reading +1 as “wins by one”. It means your team can lose by one and you still get your stake back.
  • Forgetting that quarter lines can pay or lose only half.
  • Comparing odds on different lines. 1.90 at -0.5 and 1.90 at -1 are not the same bet.

Frequently asked questions

What does Asian handicap -0.5 mean?

Your team must win the match for the bet to win. A draw or a defeat loses.

What does Asian handicap +0.5 mean?

Your team wins the bet if it wins or draws. Only a defeat loses.

What is a half win in Asian handicap?

On a quarter line, half your stake wins and half is returned, so you profit but get less than a full win.

Is Asian handicap better than 1X2?

It is different, not better. It removes the draw as a separate outcome, but the odds change to reflect that, so the bookmaker margin still applies.
