Brazilian Serie A predictions: late-night fixtures in WAT

Brazil is four hours behind Nigeria, so a 9pm game in Brazil starts at 1am on a Nigerian clock. That makes the Brasileirão a late-night league for most readers, and a good one to check results for in the morning rather than stay up.

Brazilian football has long travel distances, a tight calendar and many home advantages, which show up as short home prices and a lot of draws. Use the percentages on the page and keep stakes small when you cannot watch the game. Related reading: draw predictions.
