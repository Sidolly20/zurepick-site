Eredivisie over 1.5 and 2.5 tips: why the Dutch league is a goals league

The Eredivisie has 18 clubs and 34 matches each, and it is known for attacking football and young players. That is why “Eredivisie over 1.5 and 2.5 tips” are so popular. The league has regularly produced above-average goals, particularly in matches involving the top sides.

Big clubs can win by large scores, but so can the underdogs lose by a lot, so be careful with large stakes on the Over 3.5 line. For safer goal lines read Over 1.5 predictions; for a higher-risk line see Over 2.5.
