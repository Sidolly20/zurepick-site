Football predictions in Nigerian time: WAT kick-off guide

Every time on ZurePicks is in West Africa Time. Here is how to convert the rest.

Written by A. S. Jaye, founder and editor · Reviewed 20 November 2026

What WAT means

West Africa Time is UTC+1, and Nigeria does not change its clocks for summer. That is why kick-offs shift by an hour for you twice a year even though nothing changed in Nigeria.

When are the main competitions?

Premier League games are mostly on weekends, with a busy Saturday afternoon. Champions League nights are on Tuesday and Wednesday, and Europa League on Thursday. Japan’s J1 League is a morning league for Nigerian readers and Brazil’s Serie A is a late-night one. Today’s fixtures with WAT times are on the home page, and the league pages show kick-offs too: Premier League, Champions League, J1 League and Brasileirão.

Do not miss the start

Always check the time on the card before you place a bet. Once a game starts, pre-match prices are gone and the tip is no longer useful. For games already in progress, see the live page.

When the gap with Europe changes

Europe and the UK move their clocks on the last Sunday of March and the last Sunday of October. In 2026 the clocks went back on 25 October, and in 2027 they go forward on 28 March. Nigeria does not change, so in between, a 3pm UK kick-off is 4pm in Nigeria, and a 9pm game in Spain, Italy, Germany or France is 9pm in Nigeria. In the summer the UK is on the same clock as Nigeria, and central Europe is one hour ahead of it. A kick-off time you saved last spring may be an hour out now.

A typical week in Nigerian time

  • Friday: a few league openers, mostly in the evening.
  • Saturday: the busiest day. English games from early afternoon, with Spanish, Italian and German games in the late afternoon and evening.
  • Sunday: a second big day, with late evening games from Spain and Italy.
  • Tuesday and Wednesday: Champions League, usually from 6:45pm and 9:00pm in the European winter.
  • Thursday: Europa League, with an earlier and a later slot.

How to avoid mistakes

Set your phone to Lagos time, then compare the time on the ZurePicks card with the time in your betting app before you confirm. If the two disagree by an hour, one of them is on the wrong time zone. Remember too that live prices change quickly after kick-off, so the tip you read before the game is no use once it starts.
