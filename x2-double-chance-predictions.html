When draw or away (X2) is worth a look

X2 is how you back a good away side without needing it to win. It suits matches where the visitors are the stronger team but the game could easily be tight, such as a derby, a cup second leg or a table-topper going to a side that defends deep. The draw is your insurance.

Where it goes wrong

Because away favourites are rarer, the temptation is to scrape the bottom of the list. Do not. An X2 pick below our bar was priced as a coin flip with a safety net, and over time that does not beat the margin. Also, a late team-news change on the road is common, so check the line-ups before you commit.

A worked example

A visitor priced 2.10 to win, with the draw at 3.40 and the home side at 3.70, has X2 implied at about 74%, just below our line. If the visitor shortens to 1.70, the draw moves to 3.90 and the home side drifts to 5.00, X2 rises to about 81% and the game is listed.

Frequently asked questions

What does draw or away (X2) mean?

The visitors win or it ends level. These games only show up when the market rates the away side at 75% or higher not to lose.

How do you choose draw or away (X2) picks?

We take the bookmaker’s odds for the market, remove the margin to get fair chances, and list only the games where this outcome clears our quality bar. Quiet days are normal; an empty list is better than a weak tip.

Is this guaranteed?

No. A 70% pick still loses three times in ten. Every result, win or lose, goes on the track record page.
