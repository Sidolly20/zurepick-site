When under 3.5 goals is worth a look

Under 3.5 is a more forgiving goals bet than Under 2.5: it still wins at 2-1 and 1-2. People use it as a safer anchor leg on an accumulator, or as the other half of a plan that pairs an away win with a low-scoring game.

Where it goes wrong

The prices are short, so a single 3-1 can cost you a lot of small wins. Be wary of games between two strong attacks with weak defences, where the market may still underrate the chance of a goal-fest.

A worked example

With Under 3.5 at 1.22 and Over 3.5 at 4.00, the raw chances are 82% and 25%, 107% in total. Strip the margin and the under is about 77%. That is well over the 70% bar, so the game would be listed.

We take the bookmaker’s odds for the market, remove the margin to get fair chances, and list only the games where this outcome clears our quality bar. Quiet days are normal; an empty list is better than a weak tip.
