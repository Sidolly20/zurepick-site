How to read Both teams to score (BTTS) picks (without squinting)

Both teams to score (BTTS or GG) wins when each side scores at least once, whatever the final result. A 1-1 draw and a 4-3 win both count.

Tap any odds button to drop it into your slip. The glowing price is the market favourite. Once a game ends you’ll see a ✓ or ✗, because a tip you can’t check is just an opinion. The track record has the full 30-day scorecard, and the AI picker can turn today’s best picks into a 2 odds or 3 odds slip in one tap.

When both teams to score (btts) makes sense

Both teams to score suits games where each side scores regularly and defends loosely, and where neither team is so dominant that the opponent cannot get a goal. No means one side fails to score, which is common when a strong favourite faces a poor attack or a team in a defensive mood.

Common mistakes to avoid

BTTS Yes loses to any clean sheet, including a 3-0 win, so a lopsided game can look like a goals game and still fail. Another mistake is ignoring injuries to a main striker or a first-choice goalkeeper, which can change the picture a lot.

A worked example

BTTS Yes at 1.80 and No at 2.00 imply 55.6% and 50.0%, a total of 105.6%. After margin that is roughly 52.6% for Yes and 47.4% for No, so the market sees it as slightly more likely than not. Our published picks sit well above that.

BTTS and GG predictions today: the same bet, two names

BTTS and GG are the same market. BTTS stands for both teams to score and GG means goal-goal, the term used by many African bettors. It wins when each team scores at least once, whatever the final score. 1-1 wins, 3-2 wins, 3-0 loses.

We list games where both attacks are active and the defences are not airtight. “Sure GG tips today” does not exist, but a filtered list does, and it shows the percentage behind every pick. Watch for a missing striker or goalkeeper and for heavy favourites who often win 2-0 or 3-0, because that kills the bet. For the full explanation, read what BTTS and GG mean.

Frequently asked questions

What is both teams to score (btts)?

Both teams to score (BTTS or GG) wins when each side scores at least once, whatever the final result. A 1-1 draw and a 4-3 win both count.

Are these football predictions really free?

Yes. Every pick, odds table and result is free, and you never need to sign up.

Where do the picks come from?

From the bookmakers’ own prices. We strip out their margin and back the outcome they rate most likely. The percentage is that market-implied chance. It isn’t a secret formula and it isn’t a guarantee.

Why do some games have no pick?

Because a coin flip isn’t a tip. We only publish picks at 60% or more for 1X2, over/under 2.5 and BTTS, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. In our tracked results, picks below that lost far more often, so we leave them out.

What does the percentage mean?

A 70% pick is one the market rates at roughly 70%, so it still loses about three times in ten. Treat it as a gauge of how one-sided a match looks, not a promise.

Are there sure odds or banker tips?

There are strong picks, but no sure ones. Football is built to surprise people. The strongest picks here are the ones the market rates highest, and every result is on the track record page for you to judge.

What does BTTS or GG mean?

BTTS (also called GG) means both teams to score. It wins when each side finds the net at least once, whatever the final result. A 1-1 draw and a 4-3 win both count.

How often does the both teams to score (btts) favourite win?

Over the last 30 days the favourite won 1080 of 1930 tracked matches (56%). That is a hit rate of the market favourite, not a promise for future games.

What does GG mean in football betting?

GG means goal-goal: both teams score at least once. It is the same as BTTS Yes.

What does NG mean?

NG means no goal for at least one team, the same as BTTS No.
