Over/under goals betting: over 1.5, over 2.5 and under 2.5

Goals markets are the easiest bets to understand, and the easiest to pick badly. Start here.

The lines in plain English

The “.5” is there so that nobody gets a refund. The total number of goals in the game is a whole number, so it always lands clearly on one side.

  • Over 0.5: at least one goal. Only a 0-0 loses.
  • Over 1.5: two or more goals. 0-0, 1-0 and 0-1 lose.
  • Over 2.5: three or more goals. This is the classic “goals game” line.
  • Under 2.5: two goals or fewer.
  • Over 3.5: four or more, usually a long price.

Reading the price

Over 2.5 at 1.90 implies about 52.6%. After the bookmaker’s margin is taken out, the real figure is a little lower, around 50%. So you’re backing a game that the market sees as a coin flip. Over 1.5 at 1.30 implies about 77%. It wins more often, and pays less, which is why it turns up in so many accumulators.

What pushes a game over or under

  • Attack against defence. Two teams that score freely and defend loosely are a classic over. Two cautious sides are not.
  • What’s at stake. Late-season relegation games and tight cup ties often go under.
  • Mismatches. A big favourite against a weak side can go over on its own, because one team does the scoring.
  • Team news. A missing keeper or striker can move a line more than the table suggests.

Pick the line that matches the game

If you expect goals but only one team to score them, over 1.5 is the cleaner choice. If you expect a proper open game, over 2.5 is the one. If you think it will be tight, under 2.5 or a both teams to score “No” may fit better.

Check the league first

Leagues are different. Some produce goals all season, some barely two a game. Each of our league pages shows the goals profile of that competition from the games we have tracked, including how often over 2.5 and BTTS have landed. It’s a better starting point than a gut feeling.

Remember that the sample on any page is finite, and no line is a sure thing. 18+ only: play responsibly.

Frequently asked questions

What does over 2.5 goals mean?

The game needs three or more goals in total. Two goals or fewer and the bet loses.

Is over 1.5 safer than over 2.5?

Yes, it wins more often because it only needs two goals, but the price is shorter, so the return is smaller.

Do extra-time goals count?

In most bookmakers’ standard goal markets only the 90 minutes plus stoppage time count. Always check the rules for cup games.
