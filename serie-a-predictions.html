Serie A predictions this weekend: patient football, patient betting

Serie A has 20 clubs and a reputation for organised defending. That tends to make low-scoring games and draws more common than in some other leagues, so Under 2.5 and double chance often come up in our lists. The big clubs also lose points in places that surprise casual bettors.

Games are spread across Friday to Monday, and kick-off times vary through the day, so check the WAT time on each card. Remember that late-season games can be affected by what teams need, not just how they play. See draw predictions and X2 for related angles.
