How to read football odds

Odds look like a code until someone hands you the key. Here’s the key.

Decimal odds in ten seconds

Decimal odds tell you what a winning bet pays back per 1 staked, stake included. Odds of 2.00 pay back 2 for every 1 you put in. Odds of 1.50 pay back 1.50.

Turning odds into a chance

Divide 1 by the odds. 2.00 means 1 ÷ 2 = 50%. 1.50 means about 66.7%. 4.00 means 25%. That’s the market’s opinion of how likely the result is.

Why the numbers never add up to 100%

Add the chances for home, draw and away and you’ll land a little above 100%. The extra is the bookmaker’s margin, their built-in fee. We strip it out before showing our percentages, which is why our picks use “chance from odds” and not the raw maths.

What this means for you

Short odds mean the market sees the result as likely, but you earn little. Long odds pay more because the result is rarer. Nothing is free either way. See how we use this on our how it works page.

Frequently asked questions

What do decimal odds of 1.80 mean?

A winning 1 bet returns 1.80 in total. The implied chance is about 55.6%, before the bookmaker’s margin is removed.

What is the bookmaker’s margin?

It’s the small extra built into every set of odds so the bookmaker profits over time. It’s why the chances for all outcomes add up to a bit over 100%.
