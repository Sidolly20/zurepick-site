How the league pages work

Each league page lists the games from the last and next three days, with the bookmaker’s price, the chance it implies once the margin is removed, our tip where one clears the bar, and the result once the match is over. Where we have a table, you will also see standings, form and goal trends, so you can judge a pick before you use it.

Which league should you follow?

The big five (Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1) have the deepest data and the sharpest prices. Smaller leagues, cup ties and international games can be harder to read because the bookmaker has less information too, so we are stricter about what we publish there. If you follow African football, start with the Nigeria Premier Football League.

Why some league pages look empty

During international breaks, cup weeks and off-seasons a league may have no games in our window. That is normal. The page still shows recent results and fills back in as soon as fixtures are published. You can always see today’s full list on the home page, or check the weekend predictions.
