The two-leg classic

A 2 odds slip is the calm one in the family. Usually it’s two games priced around 1.40 each, or one strong favourite paired with a shorter price. Doubling your money on a single coin flip is a gamble; getting there through two well-backed picks is a plan.

How we build the 2 odds slip

We look at every game with a published price that day, keep the picks the market rates at 60% or more (75% for double chance and over 1.5), then test the combinations and keep the one that lands closest to 2.00 with the best combined chance. Fewer legs usually means a better chance, so we start small and add games only when we have to.

Using it sensibly

Stake an amount you could shrug off. Remember, two 70% picks together land about 49% of the time, which is why every slip shows its combined chance. Want a different mix? Frequently asked questions

Is a 2 odds slip a good bet?

It’s a popular target because it needs very few legs, but no slip is safe. Two 70% picks together land only about 49% of the time, so stake small.

Can the two games come from different leagues?

Yes, and they often do. We look at every game with a published price that day and pick the pair with the best combined chance.

How often does the slip change?

Prices move, so the slip is rebuilt about every 20 minutes. A fresh one appears each morning for that day only.

Where do the picks come from?

From the bookmakers’ own prices. We strip out their margin and back the outcome they rate most likely. The percentage is that market-implied chance. It isn’t a secret formula and it isn’t a guarantee.

Why do some games have no pick?

Because a coin flip isn’t a tip. We only publish picks at 60% or more for 1X2, over/under 2.5 and BTTS, and 75% or more for double chance and over 1.5. In our tracked results, picks below that lost far more often, so we leave them out.
